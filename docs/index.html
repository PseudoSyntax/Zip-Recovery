Zip‑Recovery

Recover the password to a .7z or .zip archive, entirely in your browser. One archive at a time.

Your archive and wordlist never leave this page. They are read locally and tested in your browser. The only thing downloaded is the decryption library code, from a CDN. Nothing is uploaded.
